# Code of conduct

## Our standards

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Assume good intent, discuss the work rather than the person, and make space for contributors with different experience levels and backgrounds.

Examples of welcome behavior include:

- giving actionable technical feedback;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them;
- respecting privacy and removing personal data from reports;
- accepting maintainer decisions about project scope and safety boundaries.

Harassment, insults, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, publishing another person's private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile rather than opening a public issue. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates these standards.

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces managed by the project.
